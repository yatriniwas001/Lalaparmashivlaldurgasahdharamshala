About the dharamshala

A straightforward place to stay in the heart of Tallital

Lala Parma Shiv Lal Durga Sah Dharamshala is a dharamshala in Tallital, Nainital, on Nainital Road. It offers travellers, families and pilgrims visiting Nainital simple accommodation close to the lake and the town's main bus and taxi points.

This website is maintained by the management as an official point of reference. We publish only information we can confirm: our address, our phone number and our public Google rating. Everything else, including room options, timings and tariff, is confirmed with you directly when you call.

If you are planning a visit, the quickest and most reliable way to check availability is a phone call to +91 79961 93849 .
